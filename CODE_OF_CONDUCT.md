# Code of Conduct

EclipseOS is built in the open by people who disagree about details and agree
about wanting a good desktop. This code keeps that productive and kind. It
applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone
speaks for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Critique the work, not the person. Say what you would change and why.
- Welcome newcomers. A first pull request is a gift; answer it like one.
- Keep reports and reviews specific: what happened, what you expected, how to
  reproduce it.
- Respect people's time. Short, clear, and patient beats long and sharp.
- Accept that maintainers make final calls on scope and direction, and that
  they will explain them when asked.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning language, including "jokes".
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure on maintainers to merge.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call hostile in a professional
  setting.

## Security reports

Vulnerabilities are not a conduct matter. Report them privately as described
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and expect the same courtesy back.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block or ban
people who break this code. When it is practical they will say what was
wrong first.

To report a conduct problem, email **chase.brown.dev@gmail.com** with links
and a short account. Reports are read by the project owner, kept private, and
handled fairly. If the report concerns the owner, say so in the subject and
the reporter will be told who else can look at it. Retaliation against someone
for reporting is itself a violation.

Consequences are proportionate: a private note, a warning, a temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban for serious or repeated harm.
