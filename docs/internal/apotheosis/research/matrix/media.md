## Open questions

- No-C rule vs rav1d asm (nasm, cc): proposed rule amendment A in gap-media-deps-vs-no-c-rule (no C-family source compiled; nasm exception in the helper crate; committed bindings for cros-libva; pulseaudio crate instead of pipewire-rs). Owner decision still needed to adopt it.
- MP4 demux: does symphonia-format-isomp4 expose video packets and codec config? Needs a spike.
- Hardware path order: VA-API first (broad codecs) vs Vulkan Video (zero-copy, H.264 only); how does fallback pick per codec?
- Frame return format: dmabuf vs memfd per GPU/driver, and import into wgpu without a copy.
- Kitty animation and `mpv --vo=kitty` paths are fixed decisions but absent from these rows; they belong to the graphics/protocol categories.
- Which shipping terminals (if any) have native video playback? Not established by the inputs; "Seen in" is design-only.
