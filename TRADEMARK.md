# Trademark policy

The **EclipseOS** name and the project logo are **not** licensed by the AGPL or
by Apache-2.0. Copyright licences cover code; they do not grant rights in a
name. This is standard practice for open-source projects (Firefox/Iceweasel,
Red Hat/CentOS): forks may use the code, not the brand.

## What you may do without asking

- Use the software, for anything, under the AGPL.
- Say truthfully that your product uses, runs on, is built with, or is
  compatible with EclipseOS.
- Use the name in articles, talks, reviews and comparisons.
- Redistribute unmodified builds under the name.

## What requires permission

- Naming a **modified** distribution or fork "EclipseOS", or a name confusingly
  similar to it.
- Using the name or logo as, or in, your own product name, company name,
  domain name, or app-store listing.
- Any use implying endorsement, affiliation, or official status.

If you fork and change the software, please rename it. Keep an accurate
attribution line ("based on EclipseOS") — that is exactly the use the section
above permits.

## Status — read this before relying on it

A USPTO wordmark search for "EclipseOS" returned no results (2026-09-05). That
is **not a clearance**: it does not cover the registered "ECLIPSE" marks in
software classes, and infringement turns on likelihood of confusion, not exact
string match. This is an accepted risk for a personal project. A professional
clearance search is required before the project takes organisational money.

Open: whether to file for the mark, and whether to hold it personally or in an
entity.
